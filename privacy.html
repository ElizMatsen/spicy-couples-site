Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains how the Spicy Couples Game app (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles your information. In short: we do not collect, store or share any of your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your phone only:

  • The two partner names you enter.
  • Your chosen app language.
  • Custom decks and custom dice you create.
  • Whether you have finished the welcome screen.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else.

2. Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers and no internet features. It does not include:

  • Analytics or usage tracking.
  • Advertising or ad tracking.
  • Location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • Third-party SDKs that collect personal data.

We do not sell or share any data, because we do not have any.

3. When you contact us

If you email us for support, we will receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and delete it when it is no longer needed. We never share it.

4. App Store

The app is downloaded through Apple’s App Store. Apple may collect information about downloads and purchases under its own privacy policy, which we do not control. Apple may share anonymous crash and usage statistics with us only if you choose to allow this in your device settings.

5. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking on this website.

6. Deleting your data

All app data lives on your device. To delete it, simply delete the app. This permanently removes your names, settings, custom decks and custom dice.

7. Age requirement

The app contains adult content and is intended only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we do not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete. Your data on your device is fully under your control.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the app update notes.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info.spicycouplesgame@gmail.com.
